# Storyboard - Workspace promo

This video tells teams that the tools they juggle fit in one app they host
themselves. 128 BPM, 30 bars (56 s). Scene lengths, cuts and music sections
live in `scripts/promo_video.py`; the frames below are the dispatch plan.

## Frame 1
status: built
src: compositions/hook.html
type: pain_point
blueprint: overwhelm-surround + kinetic-beat-slam
beat: 4 bars, no drums. "Files here. / Chat there. / Mail? Elsewhere. / Nine apps. / Nine logins. / Nine bills." slam in place while nine generic app tiles pile in around them, then close in and glitch.
