{% comment %} Line Chart ========== Renders the geometry produced by workspace.common.charts.line_chart as an inline SVG, with a legend above it. Filled series (a cumulative flow's bands) draw a translucent polygon under their line; dashed series (a burndown's ideal) are reference lines, drawn half-transparent and without markers. Parameters: ----------- - chart: the dict returned by line_chart() - aria_label: accessible description of what the chart shows Every coordinate in `chart` is a pre-formatted string and the template does no arithmetic on purpose - see the docstring in charts.py. Colour comes from the series' `css_class` (a stroke-* utility) on the line and the legend swatch, and from `fill_class` (a fill-* utility) on the area. The polyline itself sets fill="none" as an attribute, which a fill-* class would override - hence the two separate classes. {% endcomment %}
