Find the people in your photos

Turn this on and Workspace looks for faces in your photos, then groups the ones that look like the same person, so every photo of someone is one click away.

  • What is computed: where each face is in a photo, a small picture of it, and a numeric description of its features that lets faces be compared. That description is biometric data.
  • Where: on this server only. No photo and no face is sent anywhere.
  • Which photos: your own photos in Files. Group folders and photos shared with you are left out.
  • Turning it off deletes every face, every group and every face picture right away. Your photos are not touched.
